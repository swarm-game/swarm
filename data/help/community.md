Get involved with others playing and developing Swarm!

- Meet other players and developers, ask questions, *etc.* on [Discord](https://discord.gg/kp8MuSgkPw).
- Collaboratively create documentation, show off cool robot programs,
  screencasts, speedruns, or whatever on the [Swarm
  Wiki](https://github.com/swarm-game/swarm/wiki).

Contributing
============

If you're interested in contributing to Swarm, check out our
[contributing guide](https://github.com/swarm-game/swarm/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). All are welcome!

Swarm is written in Haskell.  Even if you don't know any Haskell, you can still contribute bug
reports, [designs for new challenge scenarios](worldref), or documentation.
The documents in this help system are all written in [Markdown format
(with a few extensions)](markdown), in the
[`data/help`{=snippet}](https://github.com/swarm-game/swarm/tree/main/data/help)
folder.
